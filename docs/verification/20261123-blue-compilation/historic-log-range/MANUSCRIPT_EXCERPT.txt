104: For logarithms put $z=(x-1)/(x+1)$.  If $1\le x\le2$, then
105: \begin{equation}
106:  2\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\frac{z^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
107:  \le\log x\le
108:  2\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\frac{z^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
109:  +\frac{2z^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)(1-z^2)}.
110:  \label{num:log-enclosure}
111: \end{equation}
112: This follows by summing the positive power series for
113: $2\operatorname{arctanh}z$ and bounding the remaining denominators
114: below by $2n+1$.  For $n=96$ the remainder is at most
115: $9/(4\cdot193\cdot3^{193})$.  Range reduction and a separate
116: enclosure of $\log2$ extend the formula to every positive rational
117: argument used here.  The paired-cell calculation uses denominator
118: $2^{224}$ for the series and denominator $2^{160}$ for its returned
119: intervals.  Thus no floating-point evaluation of a logarithm determines
120: the direction of an inequality.
