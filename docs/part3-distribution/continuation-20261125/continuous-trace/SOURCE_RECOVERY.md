object databases; 30 distinct ZIP inventories; and a reconstructed

archive bytes. Seven relevant saved archives were inspected, including

## Consequence

No one of these six sources was recovered from the checked scope.
This does not claim absence from all archives, private workspaces or
historical caches, and does not retract their prior registration.
The original-row adapter remains source-only until compatible source
dependencies are restored and compiled. The independently accepted
continuous Cauchy core does not certify that adapter or the full P3.5
kernel estimate.
