## New complete W layer

The reused constant is `P2GammaU5PlateauGeometry.plateauLevel = B(a) = 90621/160000`.
On the original triangle `a <= u < t < h`, the new geometric theorem states

`level u t = plateauLevel <-> t <= 10621/80000 and 17293/80000 <= t+u`.

Both boundary lines belong to the level set. The proof uses `v(t) = A(t)`
and `A(t+u) > plateauLevel`, so the remaining minimum can reach the level
only through its C branch. In exact rational arithmetic,
`A(2*a) - plateauLevel = 2723/160000 > 0`.

`wLayer N` is a filter of the literal original `pairSupport N`, using those
two coordinate gates. For N >= 2 it equals `NativeRootCover.marks N plateauLevel`.
The complement remains inside that same support and is characterized exactly by
the disjunction of the two strict reverse inequalities. The original target
exclusion and ordering of the prime pair are preserved.

The native policy equality and both original integer root-square gates transfer
to this layer, with the unchanged buffer seed `plateauLevel - 2*b` and
`rootQ N p = N^plateauLevel`. The two signed partition declarations express the
same exact partition in row and existing level-cover forms. Both retain every
complementary row with its original selected policy. The expanded cover also
retains the nonzero-coefficient filter and full modulus `(p.1*p.2)*k`.

Fixed level and seed do not make the whole policy constant: `policyAt` still
depends on N and the original pair p. Moreover, `rootRoute` assigns equality
ties to V. The closed geometric level set retains those equality boundaries;
no converse identification with `rootRoute = none` is asserted here. No
arithmetic existence claim for equality points in `pairSupport` is made.

## Obligations retained

The new layer is not a complete finite family of fixed physical root levels for
all original U5 pairs. Its complement includes regions of positive area; it
cannot be discarded as a vanishing mesh boundary. Auxiliary covers and the
above wordwise gates do not establish physical family adoption, complete fresh
and W factorization licences, a whole-atom tensor identity, complete (S)/(F),
the required sparse/query-family estimates, or PP.

The positive selected-policy pair-weighted signed full-density bound also remains
open. The manuscript's numerical lower-root certificate estimates an actual
nonnegative marked-count functional. It does not identify that functional with
an arbitrary selected-policy divisor density. Existing ordinary-density limits,
finite certificate data and arithmetic L5 aggregation are separate statements;
an actual policy comparison and a paid disagreement budget are still required.
