## Absolute convergence

Fix the manuscript Fourier mode \(\ell\), \(Y>0\), and \(\Phi(q)=\mathrm{spectralTest}(\ell,Y,q)\). For real \(t\), let

\[
 H_\Phi(t)=8\cosh(\pi t)\int_0^\infty K_{2it}(q)\Phi(q)\,\frac{dq}{q}.
\]

The log pullback \(r\mapsto\Phi(e^r)\) is a compact smooth test. Transferring the actual radial Hamiltonian gives \(\|H_\Phi(t)\|(1+|t|)^5\le D_{\ell,Y}\) on both tails and at \(t=0\). The lower-band argument uses the directly bounded compact test. For real \(\alpha\in[0,1/2]\), the raw integral \(8\int K_{2\alpha}\Phi\,dq/q\) is absolutely convergent and uniformly bounded in \(\alpha\). In the Laplace representation the positive support floor \(a=Y/16\) gives the common integrable bound \(\exp(-a\cosh u+u)\) on \(u>0\).
