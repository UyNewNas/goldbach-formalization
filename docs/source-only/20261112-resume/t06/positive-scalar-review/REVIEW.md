# The first small-height limit for positive scalar packets

Let \(\eta,h\) be smooth compact logarithmic tests with \(\int\eta=1\) and \(\int h=0\). For \(s=\log(ab)+R\) and \(w=\tfrac12\log(a/b)\), the two actual spatial Fourier coefficients contribute \(4K_\nu(2e^{L+w})K_\nu(2e^{L-w})\), where \(L=\log\pi+(s-R)/2\). The weight in the integral is \(\eta(s)h(w)\,ds\,dw\). Each spatial coefficient has order \(\nu=it\) on the regular branch and \(\nu=\alpha\) on a complementary branch. The single Bessel function on the geometric side of the eventual Kuznetsov transform uses the double order. [Original two height probes](https://github.com/UyNewNas/goldbach-formalization/blob/f16653991419f0d20e64d8639a102833683d9794/docs/source-only/20261110-yellow-closure-10t/t08_actual_spectral_supplier/Part3OriginalContinuousPoincarePackets.lean#L41-L52)

For purely imaginary order the positive-argument power \((q/2)^{it}\) has absolute value one. The normalized \(I\) series and the exact two-wave coefficient bound give \(|K_{it}(q)|\le |\Gamma(it)|A_B\) for every \(0<q\le B\) and \(t\ne0\). The constant needs an upper bound on the physical argument alone. With \(|\Gamma(it)|^2=\pi/[t\sinh(\pi t)]\), the product multiplied by \(\cosh(\pi t)\) is \(O_B(1/|t|)\) on \(|t|\ge1\). [Physical Gamma identity](https://github.com/UyNewNas/goldbach-formalization/blob/ca092596f3612af8b4da25b279ce9210091ee0cb/ImbalancedChen/Part3ImaginaryGammaNormalization.lean#L63-L69)

For \(x\le0\), the normalized series \(\Gamma(1+\nu)I_{\mathrm{core}}(\nu,e^{2x})\) has constant term one and remainder \(O(e^{2x})\) uniform on \(\Re\nu\ge-3/4\). The original positive-real, negative-real and purely imaginary order connection formulas cover \(-1<\Re\nu<1\), \(\nu\ne0\). On \(|\nu|=3/4\) the two leading waves \(\tfrac12\Gamma(-\nu)e^{\nu x}+\tfrac12\Gamma(\nu)e^{-\nu x}\) are \(O(e^{-3x/4})\). The error for the actual \(K_\nu(2e^x)\) is \(O(e^{5x/4})\). Compactness of this particular order circle bounds both Gamma factors.

The product centered at \(w=0\) cancels both equal-exponent waves before any integration. The remaining two waves give \(4\Gamma(\nu)\Gamma(-\nu)\sinh^2(\nu w)\). Its removable form is

\[
 -4\Gamma(1+\nu)\Gamma(1-\nu)w^2\left(\frac{\sinh(\nu w)}{\nu w}\right)^2.
\]

The source uses the derivative-based divided difference of the entire \(\sinh\) at zero. For \(|w|\le U\), \(L\le-U\), multiplying the one-wave estimates gives an error bounded by \(24C^2 e^{2U}e^{L/2}\) on the circle. The exact centered product is entire in the order. The removable expression is holomorphic in \(|\nu|<1\). The maximum modulus principle gives the same error on the whole closed \(3/4\) disk, including zero and \(0\le\alpha<1/2\). [Holomorphy of the actual K](https://github.com/UyNewNas/goldbach-formalization/blob/ca092596f3612af8b4da25b279ce9210091ee0cb/ImbalancedChen/Part3ComplexOrderKEntire.lean#L218-L220) [Maximum modulus on a bounded domain](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/e4c91783ca8e6a7c693ae624ade32fd22d4e43c1/Mathlib/Analysis/Complex/AbsMax.lean#L403-L408)

In the packet the subtracted center depends only on \(s,R\). Its integral is zero by Fubini and \(\int h=0\). The unit mass of \(\eta\) leaves the integral of the limiting kernel against \(h\). The fixed compact supports give an integrated \(O_{\eta,h}(e^{-R/4})\) error uniformly on the order disk. For each nonzero fixed order with \(|\Re\nu|\le3/4\) outside the disk a separate two-wave bound proves pointwise convergence. At zero the answer is \(-4\int w^2h(w)\,dw\). At nonzero order it is \(2\Gamma(\nu)\Gamma(-\nu)\int h(w)\cosh(2\nu w)\,dw\).

The linear change \((s,w)\mapsto(x,y)=(s/2+w,s/2-w)\) preserves Lebesgue measure. It is realized by two measure-preserving shears and a coordinate exchange. The compact function \(F(x,y)=\eta(x+y)h((x-y)/2)\) has fixed support as its radial arguments shift by \(c=\log(2\pi)-R/2\). The original Hamiltonian transfer in the \(x\) variable multiplies the transform by \(t^2\) and replaces \(F\) by \(-\partial_x^2 F+e^{2(x+c)}F\). For \(R\ge0\) this has a common compactly supported \(L^1\) majorant. The preceding product estimate gives \(\cosh(\pi t)|\operatorname{packet}_{\eta,h,R}(it)|\le C_{\eta,h}|t|^{-3}\) for \(|t|\ge1\). Coupled to the fixed-level \(O_{N,m}(1+T)\) unit-strip mass this yields a summable real-frequency tail at a fixed ratio window.
