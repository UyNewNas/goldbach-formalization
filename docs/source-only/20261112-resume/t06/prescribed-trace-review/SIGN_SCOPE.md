# Sign of the original complete Fourier return

In the original `fullPieceMode` the summand at each key \(k\) and common interval \(j\) contains both `fullBlockMode ... false` and `fullBlockMode ... true`. The two Boolean choices here are the signs of the Poisson frequency. Each such block also contains the two fixed-\(Z\) charts. The published reconstruction inserts these full pieces into the Fourier sum for `smoothSix`, together with its separately identified remainder. [Both Poisson signs and both charts](https://github.com/UyNewNas/goldbach-formalization/blob/32d541884c85748e3fb18f7a83801b2f8b16e359/docs/source-only/20261102-resume-30r/full_modulus_test_support/Part3OriginalFullModulusAssembly.lean#L202-L238) [Original six-piece reconstruction](https://github.com/UyNewNas/goldbach-formalization/blob/32d541884c85748e3fb18f7a83801b2f8b16e359/docs/source-only/20261102-resume-30r/full_modulus_test_support/Part3OriginalFullModulusAssembly.lean#L248-L258)

For each \(p\) in the common interval, \(p\ge2^j>0\). The involution on the Kloosterman sum makes this first index positive. The two possible second indices at a fixed \(z,n\) are \(h_0=a\,y\) and \(h_1=-a\,y\), with \(y=(\operatorname{decode}(k,z,n))_2\). The assumption on the residue and the proved positivity on `nCarrier` give \(|h_0|=|h_1|=|a|k_1 n>0\). Therefore every active \(p,n\) occurrence supplies exactly one pair \((p,h)\) with \(h>0\) and one with \(h<0\). The Boolean `traceNegative` incorporates the residue, primary sign and Poisson sign. It is constant over each such row. The phase in `traceBeta` has unit norm, and the completed \(p\) phase and the test are retained. The support proof concerns the original frequencies. Arithmetic cancellation or particular zero data can still annul individual contributions. The opposite-sign `K` identity covers the explicit restriction `traceNegative ... = true`, including the off-diagonal situation at level one. The positive-\(h\) part of `fullPieceMode` has the same-sign transform. The manuscript's cited cumulative estimate includes holomorphic, Maaß and continuous coefficients and uses height \(k-1\) for the holomorphic term. A complete prescribed-test spectral return along this route still needs the original same-sign branches in this trivial-character setting, with their own fixed-\(\Phi\) transforms, weights and any applicable diagonal. A statement for arbitrary characters or test families is outside this requirement.
