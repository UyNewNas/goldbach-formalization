The licensed weights are fixed finite smooth labelled-prime families with lower prime cutoff, product $O(N)$, the specified derivative control, and no extra arithmetic filter. It freezes the causal policy, retains both types of square constraints, and factors the very same physical coefficient. The ordered smooth chart uses the complete-product scale. The proof calls for the rough-slot auxiliary Siegel–Walfisz estimate, medium, low-rank and long branches, and control of trim, Fourier and squarefree-comparison losses.
