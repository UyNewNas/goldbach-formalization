## Mathematical identity at the consuming theorem

Kim and Sarnak, Appendix 2, Proposition 2, gives the exponent 7/64 at unramified finite and infinite places of a cuspidal automorphic representation of GL₂ over the rationals. Their eigenvalue corollary treats congruence subgroups. On each actual exceptional cuspidal eigenspace this gives λ≥975/4096 and τ=2√(1/4−λ)≤7/32, including nonsquarefree Γ₀(z). At finite p dividing A=|a|ν, the manuscript's coprimality makes the representation unramified. The normalized prime-power Hecke coefficients are sums of r+1 Satake monomials at pʳ. Multiplicativity yields |λ(A)|≤d(A)A^(7/64). A Lean transfer needs the identification with the concrete Hecke-normalized newform and oldform decomposition as well as the cusp Laplacian. The internal `Part3SpectralParameterMeasures` lines 124–130 explicitly ask for the genuine a.e. lower bound. Lines 142–154 restrict the measure to the desired spectral interval. Neither statement proves the original measure has zero discarded mass. [Kim and Sarnak, Appendix 2](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0894-0347-02-00410-1) · [Primary text](https://www.academia.edu/51759847/Functoriality_for_the_exterior_square_of_operatorname_GL_4_and_the_symmetric_fourth_of_operatorname_GL_2_)

The original source is Medium §10(v–vii), with the deductions to (24) at lines 349–380, Classical §§2–4, and the fixed-d packet audit at lines 117–157. I checked the following quantifiers against the papers.

- Pascadi A bounds a complex sequence at one fixed level and an actual cusp of parameter 1/z. The paper allows X≪max(1,z/L,z²/L³), so the narrower manuscript range is covered. J averages actual levels in a complete dyadic range with identity scaling at infinity, one fixed phase, M,Q≥1/2, and X≪max(M,Q²/M). The existing native interval completion keeps that single phase across both level intervals, and converts the squared K-coefficient normalization by 1/4. An absolute estimate with the stated right side still needs a formal supplier. [Pascadi, Theorems A and J](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.04239v3)
- Drappeau Proposition 4.7, (4.23)–(4.25), and Pascadi Lemma H bound the cumulative holomorphic, Maass and singular-cusp continuous quadratic forms for general dyadic coefficients. The manuscript needs the common height cutoff T≥1, both permitted signs, dyadic length ≥1/2, trivial character and the level-uniform constant. Translation requires the original coefficient R(n)=√|n|ρ_ABL(n), the holomorphic weight factors, and the single 1/(4π) and dt/cosh(πt) in the chosen trace convention. The native cuspidal projection, critical scattering and continuous removal do not furnish these absolute ℓ² bounds or the full quotient trace. The signed Bessel-transform estimates from Lemma D are a separate input. [Drappeau, Proposition 4.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.05549v4) · [Pascadi, Lemmas D and H](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.04239v3)

For s=1, (a,z)=1 and ν supported on primes of a force K=1. The marked coefficient after canceling the original phase is 1₍ₙ,ₐ₎₌₁ β(νn,z)e(−n/z) in the positive-sign convention. The finite-place exponent enters before squaring, yielding the manuscript's 7/32 cost on the Maass side. The paper removes that power for holomorphic and continuous forms.
