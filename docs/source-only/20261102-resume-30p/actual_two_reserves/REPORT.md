The single-policy upper projection needs only the proved nonnegative continuation correction. It can remain in the exact ledger with a minus sign and then be discarded favorably in an upper estimate. A theorem that this correction itself is small is unnecessary and is not provided.

There is exact contrary evidence against a policy-independent zero-correction shortcut: O's `stopFirstOdd_rawDensity` and `stopFirstOdd_correction` prove, for the policy stopping at its first odd node, `F_full = F_presieved = 1` and `correction = 1 - V(w)`.
