## Reuse and limits

I compared Medium §§7,10(vii), lines 349–380/468–483; the original S1 spectral return lines 168–192/203–244; the classical substitution note lines 24–50; the saved Pascadi v3 source, Theorem J (3.34)–(3.35) and its uniform phase qualification; and Drappeau pp. 16–18, Lemmas 4.9–4.11. Pascadi's full-range theorem is a legitimate paper input on the interval and ONB in question. Its proof on the named Lean coefficients remains a substantive formal obligation. The endpoint-corrected scales obey W≤4max(M,Q²/M) on both level pieces. This is not an initial-range comparison: take V=Z tending to infinity to see W=V while max(1,Q/M) stays bounded.

As a matter of expression, “initial” refers here to the same native, **untwisted** mean on (Q,2Q] at small weight. The actual switching expression in Drappeau (4.29) has Q<q≤16Q and Mellin twists n^{it}; the improved interval starting estimate (4.30) also uses the inverse-residue pair bound. The cited initial terminal is not a proof of any of those. The interval convention and the phase/norm conversions are elementary, accounted changes. A proof of the whole switching/interval estimate, or a verified formal realization of the printed theorem, is still required. Moving from the actual Bessel-generated m weight to W outside its range uses the uniform spectral gap and its own loss. Likewise no raw discrete ABL, full regular large sieve, full 7/32 budget, (24)/(25), or PP is concluded here.
