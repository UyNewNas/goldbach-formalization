## Prefix identity

On the integer interval [a,b), the coefficient at i=b is bⁱᵗ. At each a≤i<b the coefficient is iⁱᵗ−(i+1)ⁱᵗ. The coefficient at i multiplies the sum on [a,min(b,i+1)). For each original m, summing its coefficients over m≤i≤b telescopes exactly to mⁱᵗ. The endpoint b is outside the original interval and appears once as the boundary coefficient. For b=a the single coefficient multiplies an empty prefix. When 0<a≤b≤2a, integrating the bound |t|/x over each unit interval gives a contribution ≤|t|/a. The sum of all coefficient norms is at most 1+(b−a)|t|/a≤1+|t|.

Apply the identity once on each of the two independent dyadic intervals. The expansion retains e(mω)e(−nω)S(m,n;k) inside the original two prefixes. For s,t real the factor paid is (1+|s|)(1+|t|), and both prefix coefficient arrays are independent of k. The previously proved all-modulus prefix bound therefore applies after the finite exchanges. A convention conjugating the m-leg corresponds to changing ω to −ω in the entire inequality. The same phase continues across the modulus sum.
