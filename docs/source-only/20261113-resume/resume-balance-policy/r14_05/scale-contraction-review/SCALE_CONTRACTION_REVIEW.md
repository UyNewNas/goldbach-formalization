## Prefixes and native order

For a finite set S, E(S,X,a,b,ω,t) sums over all z∈S and strict exceptional eigenslots at z the nonnegative weight X^τ times the square of the actual infinity row over [a,b). The row is e(ωm)mⁱᵗρᴷᵢ(m) at the identity frame. The full dyadic specialization of this expression is the published twisted energy. For 0<a≤b≤2a, Abel summation uses the same scalar coefficients for all levels and slots. Their total variation is L≤1+|t| including the endpoint. The triangle and Cauchy inequalities bound the square for one row by L times the weighted sum of prefix squares. After multiplication by X^τ and summation over all z and i, the right side is L Σᵣ|cᵣ| Eᵣ. Its bound by L² maxᵣ Eᵣ chooses a single prefix endpoint min(b,r+1) common to the full energy and preserves the given ω. This also handles empty S, a=b, X=0 and the smallest positive dyadic block.

The separate weight transport uses 0<Z≤X and the explicit law τ≤1/2 for every slot of S to infer (X/Z)^τ≤√(X/Z). A uniform native version is required when S ranges over every parent and child. Native eigenvalue positivity only gives τ<1. The earlier fixed T<1 transform bounds by themselves do not supply the numerical square-root exponent.
