# Positive exceptional transform at the switching scale

For the physical order s = 2√(1/4 − λ) and the single bump at Φ(Yx), the native positive transform including the outside 1/cos(πs/2) has real part at least cYˢ. For the Mellin-modulated test Φ(Yx)x⁻ⁱᵗ, the modulus of the full transform is at most C(1+log(4Y))Yˢ, uniformly in real t. Fix 0 ≤ T < 1, then choose c,C,Y₀ before Y ≥ Y₀, the level, the basis slot and t. These conclusions cover exactly the slots with 0 < s ≤ T.

`core_band` bounds the actual reciprocal-Gamma power series and its order derivative on a larger complex disk and retains a positive real part on the real order segment. For b = x/2, L = −log b, u = exp(−2sL), the complementary numerator is b⁻ˢ(A − uB), where A and B are the actual cores at orders −s and s. The difference of the cores is bounded by 2sK. Taking L large buys a lower bound cs/2 for the real part of A − uB and a norm upper bound 2sK(1+L). Dividing by sin(πs/2) gives the stated bounds without a singular loss at s tending to zero. The normalized single bump equals one on [1/Y,2/Y]. The full support and the Mellin factor of modulus one give the upper estimate.

`native_band` includes the original cosine denominator. `finite_native_floor` retains every native exceptional slot on every selected level, with the Pascadi energy in its original ρᴷ convention and exactly four times the trace energy in the R convention. Its threshold and constant may depend on the finite set of levels. A level-independent band containing the entire native spectrum would make the constants uniform over level sets. The numerical spectral-gap provider required for this use, the uniform regular trace errors and the full level induction remain open.
