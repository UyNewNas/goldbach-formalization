# Native trace projections on each common interval

The same-infinity positive-sign trace splits, at each positive pair of marks, into the finite strict exceptional sum, the real-parameter Maass sum including the endpoint at eigenvalue $1/4$, the complete incoming Eisenstein integral, and the positive-weight Petersson sum. The proof obtains summability of the principal projection from the two-mark native cubic price. All four contributions retain the original exterior spectral factors.

On every positive common interval $[a,b)$, the two traces have the identical complex sum after contraction with $\overline{e(m\omega)}e(n\omega)$. The source gives a `HasSum` over all returned levels for the four sectors together. The input has its actual $\Phi(q/Q)$ weight. Each complete trace has finite level support by the geometric identity. Separating the four infinite level sums individually would require more convergence information.

For the initial estimate (4.30), let $M=2^j-1/2$, $2^j\le a\le b\le2^{j+1}$, $L=(Q,2Q]$, and $0\le T<1$. Let $J_T$ be the actual native $\rho^K$ energy on that interval, retaining the exceptional orders $\tau\le T$. For every $\varepsilon>0$, positive constants $c,D,Y_0$ are fixed before the scales, endpoints, levels and phase. For $Y\ge Y_0$ and $Q>0$, the terminal inequality is

$$
c J_T(L,Y)\le D\left( ((1+M)(1+Y))^\varepsilon (M+Y)M +(Q+M^{1+\varepsilon})M\right)
      +4\sum_{q\in L}\left(|X_{>T}(q)|+|R(q)|+|C(q)|\right).
$$

Here $X_{>T}$ is the actual high-order complementary remainder and $R,C$ are the original two marked real-parameter spectra. The term $X_{>T}$ is retained with its full absolute value when extracting the lower native floor. A separately stated identity identifies $J_T$ with the complete native energy and makes this remainder zero when the actual order cap holds on the chosen finite level set.

The explicit geometric term comes from the independently audited initial trace bound. The second term pays the complete positive-weight trace through the public full-mask estimate.
