## Complementary transform

The fixed real bump Φ equals one on [1,2] and has closed support [1/2,5/2]. Its angular Mellin transform uses a negative exponent. Inversion restores the positive exponent with measure dt/(2π). `density_decay` chooses B for Φ alone and gives B/(1+t⁴), before any level, scale, additive phase or basis index. With D=dQ, Mellin inversion of Φ(4π√(mn)/(Dx)) produces (4π/D)ⁱᵗ x⁻ⁱᵗ mⁱᵗᐟ² nⁱᵗᐟ². Thus the first native row has frequency −t/2 inside conjugation, and the second has frequency +t/2.

`native_weighted_row` specializes to the T08 dyadic index set and e(mω)ρᴷ at the identity frame of Γ₀(d). The common ω is the same on each level. The left side integrates the *product* of Φ(Yx) and Φ(4π√(mn)/(dQx)) against the native complementary kernel with measure dx/x. On the right, the transform integrates Φ(Yx)x⁻ⁱᵗ against that same kernel. Fubini follows from absolute product integrability. For each row the preliminary L¹ domination uses the finite sum of absolute row coefficients and the compactly supported kernel density. The resulting identity preserves the two oscillatory row sums exactly. This preliminary absolute bound does not furnish a large-sieve estimate.

The order a=√(1/4−λ) belongs to (0,1/2) on every strict native exceptional index. The kernel includes the 2π/sin(πa) factor and the difference J₋₂ₐ−J₂ₐ. The outer 1/cos(πa) in the native spectral trace is common to the two sides for a fixed eigenvector. T08 uses ρᴷ=2R and already converts complete squared energies by the factor four. ## Arithmetic in (4.30)

`inverse_short_bound` opens the original S(m,n;k) and sums over its actual units. The first prefix receives u/k+ω and the second receives u⁻¹/k−ω. Renaming ω to −ω gives the conjugate-first convention in a spectral square. The finite sets can have independent endpoints. `prefix_bound` uses the interval length at resonance and the minimum of the length and the geometric denominator bound elsewhere. This is the U₁ ≤ U₂ step of Proof 4.7. The theorem also applies at k=1. [The original inverse-pair sums and mean](https://arxiv.org/html/1504.05549v4#S4.SS2)

## Remaining estimates

The compact-probe formula and the prescribed native transform meet at the same single real argument. To identify the two resulting sums at a new level requires a same-frame prescribed-test trace, with simultaneous uniform estimates for the regular, holomorphic and continuous remainders in the two trace applications. The published mixed-frame `original_prescribed_positive_trace` uses d√q, the coprimality gate, a diagonal only at q=1 and m=n, and its special period-64 test. Its analytic kernel and rank-one changes of variable were reused. The distinct geometric and diagonal expressions preclude substituting its terminal statement at a varying level d. A scalar completion at the two same-frame probes and extension to both prescribed cutoffs are still required. The complementary upper/lower transform envelopes and the coverage of the printed level intervals must also be priced to obtain (4.29).
