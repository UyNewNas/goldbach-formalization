# The additive error at X = 1

For any real angular Mellin frequency t the coefficients e(mω)mⁱᵗ have unit norm. Apply the signed fixed-level sieve at the identity cusp and weight X = 1 on the actual positive block [2ʲ,2ʲ⁺¹), then sum over Q < q ≤ 2Q. With M = 2ʲ − 1/2 the resulting full native ρᴷ energy is at most 32 Cₛ (Q+M)M for every Q > 0 and every t. At t = 0 this energy coincides with T08's `pascadiJ`. The factor four relating ρᴷ to R enters the proof before taking the average.

The integral of the two native baseline energies at −t/2 and +t/2 against the absolute angular Mellin density is at most 64 Cₛ (Q+M)M times the L¹ norm of that fixed density. This supplies the initial quadratic term accompanying the additive Yᵋ in Proof 4.6. The larger Yˢ term remains a switched, Mellin-twisted energy in the level induction.
