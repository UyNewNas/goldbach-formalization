# Paying the two regular terms of the exceptional level switch

Drappeau's Proofs 4.6–4.7 use a regular test with cubic decay on the real line. For the single cutoff and its reflected counterpart this gives a factor $1+\log^2(2Y)$. The two positive forms used here are the complete native $\lambda\ge1/4$ cuspidal spectrum and all incoming channels with measure $dt/(4\pi)$. The trace pairs the same-infinity Whittaker coefficients before the row is estimated. [Drappeau, Lemma 4.10](https://arxiv.org/html/1504.05549v4#S4.SS2)

The large-sieve input is the proved declaration `manuscript_weight_zero` in the independently reviewed second generation of the cumulative-measure source. Its constant depends only on the positive input exponent. Set $A=M^{1+\varepsilon}/d$. The shell $2^n\le1+|t|<2^{n+1}$ has cubic weight at most $2^{-3n}$. The cumulative sieve up to $2^{n+1}$ therefore costs at most $4C_\varepsilon(1+A)2^{-n}\sum_m|u_m|^2$ on that shell. The threshold belongs to the first shell. The disjoint half-open shells cover the entire incoming line. The discrete proof embeds each native slot at its own shell and retains the complete multiplicity.

`squares` applies the bound to the exact block with arbitrary complex coefficients and any phase. `cross` prices the two independently shifted rows in the reflected trace by their two positive squared norms. It proves integrability of the resulting complex continuous integrand and estimates its absolute integral. `reflected` sums the four-dimensional Fourier coefficients of the physical reflected cutoff using the audited mode exchange. `original` uses the finite actual marked covariance for the single cutoff. Its real-parameter weight is measurable by the proved Fourier series for the prescribed transform, including its removable value at zero.

For $M=2^j-1/2$, $2^j\le a\le b\le2^{j+1}$, $B,Q>0$, $Y\ge1$, and one common phase $\omega$, `regular_dyad` bounds
$$
\sum_{B<d\le2B}\left(\left|R(s,d,[a,b),Y,Q,\omega)\right|+
\left|C(s,d,[a,b),Y,Q,\omega)\right|\right)
\le K_\varepsilon(1+\log^2(2Y))(B+M^{1+\varepsilon})M.
$$
The constant precedes the choice of the single or reflected cutoff, the scales, endpoints and phase. Empty intervals are included. The mask is the same interval indicator used by the post-switch rows. The elementary `log_allowance` charges the logarithm to $Y^\eta$ for any fixed positive $\eta$.
