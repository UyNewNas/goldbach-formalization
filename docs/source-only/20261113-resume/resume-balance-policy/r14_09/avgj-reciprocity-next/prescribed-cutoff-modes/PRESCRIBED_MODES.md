The proof differentiates the actual jointly smooth profile in its second coordinate and uses compactness of the physical parameter interval and the fixed period cell. For each fixed r the Fourier coefficients satisfy a bound on Σ(1+|k|)^r |cₖ| that is independent of Y, both marks, the levels and Q. This follows by r+2 integrations by parts and the summable inverse-square remainder.
