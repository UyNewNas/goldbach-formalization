# Spectral and geometric sides on the same identity frame

`same_infinity_compact_trace` identifies the full positive-modulus expansion with the complete native weight-zero spectrum, for the two actual compact height probes. Both packets observe the identity frame at level q. For fixed positive indices m,n and fixed probes, the cutoff C in k is chosen before q. Each off-diagonal term contains q|k, S(m,n;k), the conjugated rank-one orbital test and the factor 1/k. The diagonal is present when m=n at every level. [Prior compact geometry and switched cutoff](../SWITCH.md)

`compact_spectrum` uses the existing quotient-space orthogonal Gram, the actual basis evaluation at the specified frame, both compact-packet residual projections and the existing native weak adjoint. Its discrete summand is the m-packet coordinate times the conjugate n-packet coordinate. Its continuous integral uses both actual Eisenstein coefficients at infinity and the complete native sum over incoming cusps with weight 1/(4π). The inputs allow independent finite coefficient rows. `native_compact_spectrum` chooses the two copies of [2^j,2^(j+1)), the same additive phase and independent real Mellin frequencies. `native_discrete_twist` compares the resulting coordinate to the previously frozen angular row with the actual factor 2 from ρᴷ=2R at every retained nonzero mode. To form the printed conjugate-first square one places its first coefficient on the conjugated leg.

The Bessel and holomorphic transforms for the prescribed product of smooth cutoffs still require extension from this compact-probe trace. The fixed-level bounds for the two regular remainders, the lower and upper exceptional transform bounds, and the subsequent level induction remain separate obligations.
