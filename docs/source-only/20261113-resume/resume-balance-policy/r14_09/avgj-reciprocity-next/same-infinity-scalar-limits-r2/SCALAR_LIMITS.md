# Same-infinity scalar tensor and the geometric limits

At level N both positive Fourier observations have cusp width one. The scalar correction is linear in the two-height product ψ(a) conjugate(φ(b)). Conjugating the compact same-infinity pretrace puts the physical coefficients in this convention. The ordinary complete Kloosterman sum is real, and the induced positive orbit equals the existing T03 induced test at 4π√(mn)/k. The diagonal is present for m=n on every level. Only its test integral is evaluated by the old level-one diagonal formula. The divisibility condition N|k and the spectral space at N remain explicit.

`full_rank_one_trace` proves the true positive-modulus sum for both compact probes. At a positive outer seed height y the lower bounds a,b on the two scaled seed supports imply an identically zero inner Bruhat kernel once 1<a k²y. The old integral identity supplies the sum from the remaining finite window. This explicit tail permits each tensor component to use its own lower height without assigning a common support floor to all unweighted component probes.

`scalar_tensor` evaluates the sum over the physical nuclear tensor of both compact spectral coordinates at the scalar two-height test. The published T04 cutoff for the *weighted* tensor and its absolute integral resolution give a common finite modulus window on the geometric side. On the spectral side the tensor sum is still the outermost sum. The true discrete and continuous sums of each rank-one component use the two identity frames at N and the actual incoming cusp measure. 

`scalar_moduli` and `scalar_limits` take the height limit at fixed window, then the window limit. Both pass through the full divisibility-restricted modulus sum. For fixed positive integer marks m,n the kth term is exactly the level-one classical arithmetic term multiplied by 1[N|k]. This uses the actual identity for the cusp-one sum at level one, where its translation phase is e(n)=1 and the inverse residue factor is one modulo k, including k=1. Its existing summable majorant can be chosen before N and both limits. The common majorant still depends on m,n and the fixed period-64 test. In the window limit the equal-mark diagonal becomes the ordinary J₀ moment divided by 2π.

To obtain the prescribed scalar trace, the outer nuclear tensor sum in `scalar_tensor` must pass through the native spectral sums, followed by the two spectral limits, retaining the same coordinates and diagonal. The geometric limits proved here do not give those spectral exchanges. The two physical cutoff tests have separate mode expansions. They require their own joint convergence in the period frequency and spectral indices after the fixed-mode trace is available.
