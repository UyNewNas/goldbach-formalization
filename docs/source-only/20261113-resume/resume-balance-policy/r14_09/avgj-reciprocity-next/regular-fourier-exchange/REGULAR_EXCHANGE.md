# Complete real-parameter spectral exchange in the returned trace

For every level $d$ of the identity observation cusp, every original dyadic block, $Y\ge1$, $Q>0$, common phase $\omega$ and arbitrary complex mask $u$, the two marked real-parameter sectors of the actual reflected $\Phi(Yx)\Phi(4\pi\sqrt{mn}/(dQx))$ trace have their full four-coordinate Fourier expansions. An indicator mask gives every common subinterval. Both shifted row frequencies are independent of the level. They are $\omega-k_1/(64M)$ and $\omega+k_2/(64M)$.

`principal_fourier` proves joint absolute summability over every Fourier mode and every real-parameter cuspidal basis slot, and a complex HasSum with the modes outermost and the complete basis sum inside. The native selector includes the boundary at Laplace eigenvalue $1/4$. The common value is the principal projection of the marked trace in its original $R$ normalization.

`incoming_fourier` gives integrability over all real $t$ at every Fourier mode, summability over the modes of the integrals of the absolute values, and a complex HasSum with the modes outermost. Its inner full-axis integral contains the sum of all incoming cusp channels and the original $1/(4\pi\cosh(\pi t))$ weight. The proof uses measurability of the actual summed native covariance and moves finite mark and channel sums algebraically. It does not require a pointwise statement about the regularity of an individual Eisenstein Fourier coefficient.

A single summable lattice envelope, including both independent mark phases and the level cutoff, bounds each radial mode by the existing cubic line weight times $1+\log^2(2Y)$. The markwise spectral integrability proof uses the same-level absolute cubic price before the finite mask is inserted. This gives the exchange on the complete real spectrum.
