# Two applications of the same-infinity prescribed trace

The tests are exactly Φ(Yx) and Φ(Yx)Φ(4π√(mn)/(dQx)). At each fixed positive scale and pair of positive Fourier marks, the compact support of the first bump gives a common finite geometric modulus range. The regular period-frequency price is polynomial at this scale, and the coefficients of either physical profile have arbitrarily many summable polynomial weights. This pays the full principal Maaß sum and the incoming Eisenstein integral at the same level. Every complementary slot is summed using its actual transform and the finiteness of the complementary subspace at that level. On the holomorphic side the common zero-mode norm of the test divided by x bounds the integer Bessel moments uniformly across all period modes. The factorial decay in the order pays the complete even-weight sum, including weight two.

`same_infinity_cutoff_trace` identifies the original sum over k divisible by q with the three native spectral components. The dependence of the double cutoff on (m,n,d) remains in each test. Each use of the full trace occurs on the actual quotient at its own integer level.

`level_switch` applies this conclusion on either side. It first chooses a common cutoff in the product k from the two marks and Y alone. Both spectra at any level above that cutoff vanish as totals by their exact geometric expansions. Inside the common finite square, the divisor change k=qd reorders the two sums. The reflected bump at the physical argument x=4π√(mn)/(qd) recovers Φ(q/Q) exactly. The terminal equates the two full sums over positive levels for every Q>0. Ranges near the dyadic transition points remain encoded by the smooth cutoffs.
