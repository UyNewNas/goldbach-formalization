# The fixed period-64 mode at the two identity frames

`weighted_hol` sums the Petersson sector at every even weight, including weight two. The full cusp-space ONB remains at level q with both observations at infinity. The joint modulus and weight majorant comes from T21's classical level-one Bessel summand after inserting the exact divisibility indicator q|k. Equality of the level-one cusp sum with the usual integral Kloosterman sum is used only in this majorant and its pointwise weighted summand identity. The normalized Fourier coefficient products are those of the original level q.

The full-basis absolute sum follows from the two same-frame diagonal bounds for the Petersson basis at q and summability of the weighted moments. The per-mark constants here include exp(4πm) and exp(4πn) from the high-strip Fourier estimate. They suffice for absolute convergence with fixed marks. They supply no uniform error estimate for moving marks.

`same_infinity_period_trace` combines the two spectral limits with the complete holomorphic projection. The J₀ collision on the scalar side and the J₀ term of the weighted Petersson formula cancel at every level. Its left-hand side is the full sum over positive k divisible by q, with S(m,n;k)/k and the original fixed period-64 test at 4π√(mn)/k. The right-hand side has the full Maaß basis, all incoming Eisenstein cusps with their native factor 1/(4π), and all even-weight cusp forms.

The first and return trace in Drappeau (4.29) require linear combinations of these fixed tests. Their Fourier coefficients and pointwise transform expansions are available in the separate prescribed-cutoff package. Passing through the infinite principal spectrum still requires a joint frequency bound. Neither the fixed-mode identity nor the absolute convergence proved here bounds either trace remainder uniformly in the level-average induction. Source-level only.
