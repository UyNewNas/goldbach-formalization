# Same-infinity Petersson formula and its positive-modulus progression

The fixed-weight identity is already present in the reviewed and published component at commit `23fa4b92744247e6e65aacb14680024df3a3fda5`. Its `same_infinity_petersson` takes the level N>0, a weight index j≥0 and two positive integer marks m,n. It sets κ=2(j+1) and uses the full specified Petersson orthonormal basis of that weight on Γ₀(N). Both Fourier evaluations use the identity frame. The proof starts with T21's arbitrary-frame denominator and its arbitrary-frame normalized Poincaré coefficient specialized to (1,1).

At the identity the bottom-row gate becomes N∣C and the residue classes that survive are units modulo C. The horizontal coefficient takes the positive-frequency phase, paired with the inverse residue from the seed. Inverting the units gives the project's S(m,n;C) in the stated order. For the zero bottom entry the horizontal projection gives δₘₙ for every N. At m=n the power of √m/√n is one. For positive m,n it cancels its reciprocal power in the orbital numerator. These operations also cover N=C=1 and j=0, the weight-two Hecke limit. The theorem has the exact fixed-weight form

    4π Γ(κ−1) Σᵢ conj(Rᵢ(m)) Rᵢ(n)
      = δₘₙ + Σ_{C>0, N∣C} (2π/C)(−i)^κ S(m,n;C) J_{κ−1}(4π√m√n/C).

The physical unscaled R coefficients and Γ factor are those of the project's T21 pretrace. The classical equation uses its own normalized Fourier coefficients. Its geometric Bessel sign and argument agree since κ is even. This is the fixed-weight step before DI's sum in (5.3) and Drappeau's comparison of the spectrum up to height T.
