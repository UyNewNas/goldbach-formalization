They are actual carrier-membership deductions, not assumptions of a contraction, stationarity or a numerical replay result.

`root_facts` derives its three root inputs from fixed box faces:

* eta<=each lower prime face;
* 6 eta<=nu-sum upper faces;

`History.family_facts` propagates these through every actual arithmetic step. There is no inference of alpha>=2 from d>0 or L>0, and no replacement of alpha by two.

`finite_book_common_floor` chooses one positive eta from the finite set of all root lower faces and residual gaps/6, and one maximum depth, before N. Rank-zero roots and an empty book are allowed.
