# Actual selected native walls

For one fixed precision, root type, chronological shape and read labels, `fullWalls` is a finite list of affine functions on the original native prime exponent coordinates. Its construction precedes the target N and the tail loss. At a point of the cube where the outer causal readout is nonzero and the original band-and-seed selection fails, `fine_outer_strip` gives a member of that list with nonzero linear part and absolute affine value at most the prescribed width. The witness concerns the point tested by the readout, even when the two original witnesses defining the mixed atom are elsewhere in its support.

## Source chain

1. SelectedQueryRows uses the original active-knot numerator to represent the physical argument by D s = g L with D positive and g fixed by the shape. Each original atom row and the Buchstab start and endpoints now have affine values. Fixed comparisons keep their own query; retained cells keep their original owner.
2. SelectedFirstChange compares two complete real walks at fixed labels. Every earlier read and strict child condition has actually passed on both points before a later changed branch is reached.
3. SelectedBranchRows lists the original strict single and pair tests. Old W1 interval and W2 single/pair catalogues are consumed directly. The strict ceiling, pair order, product cap, positive residual and upper/lower endpoint polarities all survive evaluation.
4. SelectedWallCatalogue gives the symbolic residual, denominator and ceiling recursions for every actual shape and an explicit finite list sufficient for the entire original walk.
5. SelectedNativeStrips evaluates those affine functions at the supplied native coordinates. Two different predicate values force a nonzero linear functional and the closed bound |c+Lx| ≤ ‖L‖ ‖x-y‖. A constant equality, a retained identity or any other structural row can never contribute this witness.
6. SelectedRootStrips covers both root-band endpoints, the tail cutoff, all original root-code endpoints, and both clamp pieces. On the main arm the total is min((1+x)/2,57/64−3x/2). On the U3 tail it is 1/2−tau independently of the root coordinate. An entire constant tail on a grid endpoint cannot cross an artificial wall. A changing clamp uses the actual threshold x=nu−2a. Only the finitely many original frozen endpoints nu enter the full list.
7. SelectedAtlasStrips uses the original hit/mixed predicates, atoms, mesh diameter and readout. The final quantifiers choose the mesh after the precision, fixed labels and prescribed strip width and before either point or target. Uniformity in the local tail loss follows from the same explicit list; it does not assert uniform distribution for a continuously varying family of affine intercepts.

The manuscript first fixes the finite positive parents and queries, and then lets N grow. The original common-query limit in FullMemberStrips evaluates the tail loss at finiteTau n. For that application n and finiteTau n are both fixed before N. The fixed-hyperplane arithmetic receiver can therefore be used at the original order of limits. Its later consumer must transport the native coordinates to the original old word, retaining both coordinates of W2 pairs in the producer's order; lift each nonzero old-coordinate form with zero coefficients on fresh and tail slots; and pay the finite union with the actual omitted-prime arithmetic count, including its complete-product coprimality and nonunit rough quotient. These files do not perform that marked arithmetic summation, a PP distribution estimate, or a full P2.9 closure.
